Real-time Projects

Twenty-three projects that put graphic design, video editing and AI into practice.

Project 1

Social Media Promotional Poster

Design an attractive promotional poster for a product, service, event or business. Apply typography, color theory, image placement, visual hierarchy and layout principles to create a professional design suitable for digital marketing.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva

Project 2

Brand Moodboard & Typography Design

Create a brand moodboard that communicates a business's visual identity. Select suitable fonts, colors, images and visual references, and use AI assistance to develop creative directions and maintain a consistent design style.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva, ChatGPT

Project 3

Professional Photo Manipulation

Transform ordinary photographs into creative visual compositions using selections, layers, masks, lighting adjustments and color correction. Explore AI-assisted image generation and background editing to create a polished, realistic or artistic final image.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Firefly

Project 4

YouTube Thumbnail Design — 3 Concepts

Develop three different thumbnail concepts for the same YouTube video. Experiment with typography, image composition, contrast, colors and focal points to create visually appealing thumbnails that clearly communicate the video's subject.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva

Project 5

AI-Powered Advertising Creative

Create an advertising creative for a product or service using AI-generated concepts and visual assets. Refine the images, layout, colors and promotional text to produce a professional advertisement suitable for digital campaigns.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT

Project 6

Canva Social Media Campaign

Plan and design a coordinated social media campaign for a brand. Create multiple posts using consistent typography, colors, imagery and messaging, with AI assistance for content ideas and captions.

Tools Used: Canva, ChatGPT

Project 7

Telugu Reel Scriptwriting

Write engaging Telugu scripts for short-form videos, including opening hooks, main content, scene suggestions and closing calls to action. Use AI assistance to explore ideas, improve clarity and adapt scripts for different audiences.

Tools Used: ChatGPT, Gemini AI

Project 8

Video Storyboard & Shot List

Convert a video concept into a structured storyboard with scene descriptions, shot types, visual references and a shot list. Organize the sequence of scenes and plan the visuals before starting video production.

Tools Used: ChatGPT, Canva

Project 9

Short-Form Reel Editing

Edit raw footage into a polished vertical reel. Arrange clips, remove unnecessary sections, synchronize music, improve dialogue clarity and refine pacing to create an engaging video for social media platforms.

Tools Used: Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition

Project 10

Talking-Head Video Editing

Edit an educational, interview or presentation video featuring a person speaking to the camera. Remove pauses, improve pacing, synchronize audio, balance sound levels and prepare a clean, professional final video.

Tools Used: Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition

Project 11

Cinematic Color Grading & Animated Titles

Enhance video footage through color correction, exposure balancing and cinematic color grading. Create animated titles that complement the footage and improve the overall visual presentation.

Tools Used: Adobe Premiere Pro

Project 12

Promotional Video Editing

Create a promotional video for a product, service, business or event. Combine footage, titles, transitions, background music and sound effects to communicate the key message and present the brand professionally.

Tools Used: Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition

Project 13

Professional Voice-Over & Sound Design

Produce a clear voice-over for a promotional or educational video. Improve narration quality, balance volume, reduce unwanted noise and combine voice, music and sound effects into a cohesive audio track.

Tools Used: Adobe Audition, ElevenLabs

Project 14

AI Video Generation — Prompt Experiments

Experiment with text prompts to generate AI-assisted video clips. Test different descriptions, subjects, camera movements, visual styles and lighting directions, then compare the generated results for quality and consistency.

Tools Used: Google Veo, Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT

Project 15

AI Image-to-Video Animation

Transform selected images into animated video clips using AI-assisted image-to-video workflows. Experiment with motion descriptions, camera movement and scene dynamics while maintaining the original image's visual style.

Tools Used: Google Veo, Adobe Firefly

Project 16

AI Product Advertisement

Develop a product advertisement that combines graphic design, AI-generated video and professional editing. Create product visuals, generate suitable video scenes, add narration and arrange the final sequence into a compelling promotional advertisement.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Google Veo, Adobe Premiere Pro, ElevenLabs

Project 17

AI Cinematic Storytelling Video

Develop a short cinematic story using AI-assisted visual generation and video editing. Organize scenes into a meaningful sequence, maintain visual continuity, and enhance the storytelling with music, sound effects, color grading and audio transitions.

Tools Used: Google Veo, Adobe Firefly, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition

Project 18

Complete Social Media Branding Campaign

Build a consistent social media branding campaign for a business. Develop visual concepts, select brand colors and typography, design promotional posts and prepare supporting copy that follows a consistent brand identity.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva, ChatGPT

Project 19

Corporate Client Design Project

Simulate a corporate client assignment by preparing branded graphic assets and a promotional video. Follow a design brief, maintain consistent visual identity, organize project files and deliver the final designs and video according to the specified requirements.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva, Adobe Premiere Pro

Project 20

AI-Powered Brand Promotional Video

Create a branded promotional video using AI-assisted content planning, video generation and professional post-production. Combine generated scenes, brand messaging, narration, music and sound design into a polished final video.

Tools Used: ChatGPT, Google Veo, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition

Project 21

Resume & Creative Portfolio Development

Prepare a professional resume and creative portfolio showcasing graphic design, video editing and AI-assisted projects. Organize selected work, write concise project descriptions and present the portfolio using suitable online platforms for professional visibility.

Tools Used: Canva, ChatGPT, Behance, LinkedIn

Project 22

Freelance Proposal & Client Quotation

Prepare a freelance proposal and client quotation for a design or video production assignment. Define project requirements, deliverables, timelines, pricing and revision terms, and present the information in a clear, professional document.

Tools Used: ChatGPT, Canva, Google Docs

Project 23

Final Integrated Graphic Design & Video Production

Complete an end-to-end creative project that combines graphic design, video editing and AI-assisted production. Develop the visual identity, create promotional graphics, generate or prepare video assets, edit the final video and organize the completed deliverables into a professional portfolio-ready presentation.

Tools Used: Adobe Photoshop, Canva, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition, Adobe Firefly, Google Veo, ChatGPT
